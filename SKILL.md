## Panel-specific overlay

Break the subject into several independent small hand-drawn sticker-like units that aggregate centrally into one recognisable whole. Use chunky naive shapes, clear imperfect cutout contours, overlaps and purposeful scale/direction rhythm with paper, pastel or gouache grain. Preserve warm fresh source-derived limited colours and wide surrounding whitespace. Avoid an orderly grid, random scatter, commercial sticker packs and smooth vector finish.
